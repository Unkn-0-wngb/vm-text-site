1. What the Service does

VM-Text transcribes Discord voice messages. It can do this in three ways:

  • Automatically, replying to voice messages in servers where the app has been added and automatic transcription is turned on.
  • Transcribe (private), where only the person who asked sees the result.
  • Transcribe (public), where the result is posted as a message in the channel for everyone to see.

You can change your own settings, and a server's settings if you have the Manage Server permission, with the /config command.
